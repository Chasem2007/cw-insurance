Our Story

Twenty-plus years
on Grand Avenue.

Conver & Winchell was founded in Billings by Barry Conver and Dennis Winchell in 2003. Sara McLean was an agent with the agency from the day its doors opened. After Dennis passed away, Sara became Barry’s partner in 2010. When Barry retired in 2017, she became the agency’s sole owner.

Through every chapter, Conver & Winchell has remained intentionally small. That matters to us because when you call, you should get a person who knows you—not a queue or a call center.

As an independent insurance agency, we work for our clients—not for one insurance company. We compare coverage from multiple carriers, tell you honestly when your current policy is still the right choice, and look for better options when a renewal no longer makes sense.

We insure the things that are part of life in Montana—from homes, cars, and pickups to ATVs, campers, boats, first apartments, and e-bikes. We understand the weather, roads, and everyday risks Montanans face because we live here too. That local knowledge helps us recommend coverage based on your life and your property—not generic assumptions about what you need.
