Who We Are

A small team that
knows your name.

Conver & Winchell has been an independent agency in Billings since 2003. That independence matters: we aren't employees of one insurance company trying to sell you their product. We work for you, and we shop your coverage across multiple top-rated carriers to find the fit that actually makes sense.

What makes that work is the people. We insure our neighbors' homes, our friends' trucks, and our kids' first cars, so we take getting it right personally.

Sara McLean

Owner & Agent

I was born and raised in Billings, and this area is still where my husband and I have chosen to call home and raise our family. Being local matters to me because our clients are not just policy numbers. They are neighbors, friends, and families we see throughout our community.

I have been in the insurance industry since 1998, being a part of Conver & Winchell Insurance from the day the doors opened in 2003. I became a partner in the agency in 2010 and sole owner in 2017. Over the years, I have seen insurance change a great deal, but my approach has stayed the same: offer honest guidance and help clients protect the things that matter most.

As an independent agency owner, I value the ability to work with multiple carriers and help clients find coverage that fits their needs. I am proud to lead a small, local agency where personal service still matters.

T

Tess

Office Companion & The Real Boss

Tess is our unofficial office greeter, occasional Friday visitor, and firm believer that attention should never be in short supply.

Born in 2017, Tess is a lovable mutt who became part of the Conver & Winchell office story somewhat unexpectedly. Sara used to think having a dog at the office seemed unprofessional, until Tess became an only dog in 2021 and made it very clear that being left home alone was not her preferred career path. After a few destructive reminders of her separation anxiety, Tess started coming to work with Mom.

These days, Tess is older, a little more settled, and able to stay home for longer stretches, but she still loves the attention she gets at the office. Many clients have come to know her, ask about her, and even look for her when they stop in.

Tess is proof that sometimes “never” turns into “well, maybe on Fridays.”
