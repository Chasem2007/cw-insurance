What we watch for

What Great Falls households
actually claim.

Coverage that ignores local conditions is just paperwork. Here is what shapes our recommendations in north-central Montana.

Wind, and plenty of it

Great Falls is among the windiest cities in the country. Wind-driven roof, siding, and fence claims are routine, and some policies carry a separate wind or hail deductible that people never notice until they file.

Hail alongside the wind

Storms that bring wind here often bring hail. Roof age and roof settlement terms are the two things worth reading before you buy, and we will read them with you.

Outbuildings and equipment

Shops, sheds, and detached garages are frequently underinsured because the limit was left at a default. We set those against what it would actually cost to rebuild.
